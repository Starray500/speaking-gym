My Notebook

Your mistakes and your lost words, practised out loud. Five minutes a day, and they become words you can use.

Add cards

Paste feedback from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or a teacher. A “Today's small lesson” table works too. The page finds the corrections for you.

How it works

  • Say every answer out loud. Speaking it is what moves a word from “I know it” to “I can use it”.
  • Cards come back just before you forget them: tomorrow, then in 3 days, a week, two weeks, a month…
  • Five minutes a day is enough. Do it before your other practice.
  • “Again” is not a failure. It's how your memory learns.

Where cards come from

  • After any feedback, paste it into Paste. Every correction becomes a card.
  • When you can't find a word while speaking, add it in Add as soon as you can.
  • Words you saved in Speaking Gym: use Import.
  • To bring in words you saved in Speaking Gym, use the website version of this page.

New cards a day

Fewer new cards means a shorter review. Cards that come back are always shown.
