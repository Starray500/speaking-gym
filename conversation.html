Good to know

  • Say everything out loud. Reading silently doesn't train speaking.
  • Voice typing works in Chrome and Edge. Recording works in most browsers.
  • For a live partner, copy a role-play prompt into a free AI voice chat, like ChatGPT or Gemini.
  • In role-plays, Claude plays the other person. Press the microphone to answer.
  • To record yourself, use the website version of this page.
  • After a real conversation, write it in your Brave Log, even when it felt hard.
