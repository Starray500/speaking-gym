Remember

Your accent is fine. The goal is to be understood the first time.

  • Slow down.
  • Make strong words strong.
  • Finish every word.

Good to know

  • Say everything out loud, a little louder than usual.
  • The “Say it” check uses speech recognition. It's a rough test: if the computer understands you, people probably will too. If it doesn't, try again, slower and stronger. Computers make mistakes too.
  • The microphone check works in Chrome and Edge. Recording works in most browsers.
  • To record yourself and listen back, use the website version of this page.
  • Headphones help you hear the small differences.

Your progress is saved in this browser only. Use the backup on the home page to keep it safe.
