Free help when you need feedback

These buttons are on the program pages. None of them need a subscription.

Copy a feedback prompt

Copies your text with instructions for a coach. Paste it into any free AI chat (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) and get detailed corrections.

Check grammar (free)

Uses LanguageTool to find spelling and some grammar mistakes. It doesn't catch everything.

Record and listen back

Hearing yourself is the best free teacher. Listen for long pauses and the words you searched for.

Live role-play partner

In Conversation Coach, copy the role-play prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini voice mode. Then practise a date, small talk or an interview out loud with a free AI partner.

Save your mistakes

After any feedback, paste it into My Notebook. Every “you said → better” becomes a card you practise out loud until it sticks.

Is it clear?

In Clear Voice, say any sentence and see what a computer heard. If it understands you, that’s a good sign people will too.

Practice Call

A free AI partner that runs on your Mac. Talk about anything, out loud. It corrects you gently, and you can save the fixes to My Notebook.

Stuck on a word?

Type 3–5 key words, like “proof of payment”, and a free word finder suggests the word (“receipt”).

Keep your progress safe

Your progress and your writing are saved in this browser only. If you clear your browser or change phones, they're gone. Download a backup every week. On a new device, open this page and restore it.
